Field preference

Major gets Collins wherever the bracket allows, including both championship and semifinal priority. On Sept 26 Rookie sits on Adams and Minor on Moore, so each division keeps a home base for the morning.

Weather

Thursday Sept 24 is held open on Collins and Adams for opening round makeups. Sept 26 also holds 12:00 Moore and the full 1:30 window in reserve. Do not schedule anything into either without Board approval.

Lights

Five weeknight dates carry a 7:30 kickoff, counting the Thursday hold. Sunset falls near 8:00 across that stretch, so Collins and Adams need lights confirmed for all of them.

Tower duty

Twenty five games need coverage. Weeknights are two towers per date. Sept 26 is two towers at 9:00 and three each at 10:30 and 12:00. Oct 3 is one tower on Collins for all three finals.
