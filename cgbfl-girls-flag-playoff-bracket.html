Game count

  • 2nd–4th: 5 games
  • 5th–6th: 5 games
  • 7th–8th: 3 games
  • Total: 13 games

Seeding

Seeds are set by final regular season record. Tiebreakers run in order: head to head, then points allowed in division play, then point differential capped at 14 per game, then coin flip at the Board meeting.

Bracket integrity

No reseeding after the first round. The 1 seed always draws the winner of the 4 versus 5 game and the 2 seed always draws the winner of the 3 versus 6 game, so the top two seeds stay on opposite sides of the bracket.

Ties in the postseason

Every playoff game is played to a winner. Use the league overtime format: alternating possessions from the 10 yard line, one play per possession is not used, each team gets four downs. Repeat until someone leads after equal possessions.

Schedule at a glance

  • Tue Sept 15 — Round 1, 2nd–4th and 5th–6th, 6:00p and 7:00p
  • Wed Sept 16 — 7th–8th semifinals, Moore Field, 6:30p
  • Thu Sept 17 — Semifinals, 2nd–4th and 5th–6th, 6:00p and 7:00p
  • Sat Sept 19 — All three championship games, Collins Field

Championship day

All three title games play on Collins Field back to back. 2nd–4th kicks off at 9:00a, 5th–6th at 10:00a, and 7th–8th at 11:00a.

Weather

Keep one open date after the first round for makeups. If a title game cannot be played and no date remains, the higher seed is declared champion.
