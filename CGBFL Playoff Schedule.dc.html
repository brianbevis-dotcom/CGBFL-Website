Overall game count

Thirty-eight postseason games in all: twenty five in Rookie, Minor and Major, and thirteen in girls flag — five for 2nd–4th, five for 5th–6th and three for 7th–8th.

Seeding

Seeds are set by final regular season record. Tiebreakers run in order: head to head, then points allowed in division play, then point differential capped at 14 per game, then coin flip at the Board meeting.

Bracket integrity

No reseeding after the first round. 

Tower duty

Thirty-eight games need coverage. Girls Flag are two towers each weekday, Moore and Watson Fields.  Tackle Weekdays are two towers per date. Sept 26 is two towers at 9:00 and three each at 10:30 and 12:00. Sept 19 and Oct 3 is one tower on Collins for all three finals.

OPEN SLOTS

Thursday Sept 24 is held open on Collins and Adams for opening round makeups.

Lights

Five weeknight dates carry a 7:30p kickoff, counting the Thursday hold. Lights will be on for start of all 7:30p games
